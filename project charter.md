# Sovereign Shards Project Charter

## Project Summary
Sovereign Shards is a team-driven, hands-on project where students of all experience levels collaborate to build a real-world, open-source AI toolchain. The goal is to orchestrate efficient “tiny models” on local hardware, offering a highly practical alternative to large cloud-based AI models—mirroring the resource limits and teamwork found in actual industry roles.

## Why This Matters
- **Industry Skills:** Students learn version control (git), Python collaboration, documentation, agile workflows, and secure code—all portfolio essentials.
- **Team Learning:** Mixed-experience teams let beginners learn from peers and advanced students sharpen leadership skills.
- **Mentored Growth:** The project follows structured oversight by Maestro AI and invited faculty, ensuring alignment with accredited learning standards and responsible team practices.

## Faculty/AI Oversight Commitment
We seek a dedicated faculty advisor (or periodic reviewer) to:
- Guide team process, code reviews, and learning outcomes.
- Ensure standards for code safety, project inclusivity, and academic integrity.
- Help structure “retrospectives” (quick team reviews) to continuously improve.

## How the Team Will Operate
- All onboarding, workflow, and commit processes follow best practices (checklists, teacher-approved ticketing, git hygiene).
- Maestro AI and B.L.U.E. J. will serve as day-to-day mentors for both technical help and team skills.
- Regular short updates will be shared for transparency and progress tracking.

## What We Ask
- Formal permission to proceed as an elective, portfolio, or club project (whichever fits college policy).
- Names/roles for faculty oversight or check-in support.
- **Resource allocation as needed:**
  - Access to relevant API keys (when development or integration requires it).
  - Permission to request model checkpoint availability, fine-tuning resources, or cloud compute credits (if/when faculty approve, for advanced experimentation or subject-specific training).
  - The team will coordinate with faculty on responsible use and cost awareness for all resources.

## Mission
To deliver a real, complete project that gives every member industry-ready experience, a sense of impact, and the confidence to contribute to professional teams after graduation.
